What We Enable

Two complementary
frameworks. One trusted architecture.

Both DEPA Training and DEPA Private Inferencing share a common foundation: data never leaves institutional boundaries without authorisation, purpose-bound safeguards, and cryptographic guarantees.

DEPA Training

The era of scraping public data to train AI is ending.

The next frontier demands access to high-quality datasets locked within institutional silos. DEPA Training unlocks AI model training on protected datasets — with privacy, sovereignty, and scalability built in from the start.

  • Confidential Clean Rooms — hardware-level isolation where models come to data, not data to models
  • Differential privacy with mathematical guarantees, not just anonymization
  • Electronic contracts with immutable audit trails on distributed ledgers
  • Compliant with DPDP Act, GDPR, and emerging AI regulations
Explore DEPA Training →
DEPA Private Inferencing

AI inference across institutional boundaries demands deeper trust guarantees

Privacy safeguards around sensitive institutional data remain onerous and fragile. DEPA Private Inferencing enables real-time AI inference on protected datasets without data ever leaving institutional boundaries.

  • Secure multi-party inference across organisations in real time
  • Consent-aware, purpose-limited data access at every step
  • Standardised contracts govern every cross-boundary inference event
  • Full provenance chain — from data principal to AI output

About Samyog

The trust and governance layer for India's AI data economy

Samyog is a stakeholder-led, self-regulatory organisation guiding the creation and management of Data Collaboratives — bringing together data providers, consumers, and service ecosystems to enable trusted, repeatable, multi-party data collaboration within focused domains.

It operationalises DEPA Training and Private Inferencing at scale by shaping market awareness and delivering critical governance services: certification, standardised contracts, key management infrastructure, grievance redressal, and training.

Powered by iSPIRT. Technical standards by DEPA Foundation.

Samyog Fellowship

Building India Stack's
Data Layer from the Inside

India's AI future will be shaped by those obsessed with solving problems — people willing to think independently, take initiative, and build with purpose. This is a volunteer fellowship rooted in action: a 24-week programme where you will take ownership of a module of public infrastructure — making contributions that will endure beyond the fellowship and strengthen India's digital commons.

Technical Track

Build open-source digital infrastructure for the public

Develop capabilities and components on the DEPA stack — clean rooms, privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs), and electronic contracts.

Example Projects

Confidential Clean Room components · Differential privacy implementations · Smart contract frameworks for data governance

2 Fellow spots available
Market Ecosystem Track

Enable market ecosystem players on the DEPA stack

Build and enhance the market ecosystem by expanding capabilities or creating new opportunities for data consumers and providers.

Example Projects

Data Collaborative playbooks · Sector-specific DEPA adoption frameworks · Market research and ecosystem mapping

2 Fellow spots available
A contribution lifecycle, not a course — 24 weeks
Wk 1–2
Phase One

Contextual Immersion

Learn the problem first — not just the technical or legal dimension, but why this public good matters for India. Every build decision grounded in human stakes.

Wk 3–22
Phase Two

The Build Phase

Work on a specific, scoped module — a technical component, techno-legal paper, or market enablement framework. All outputs open-source, with support from iSPIRT practitioners.

Wk 23+
Phase Three

Handover & Documentation

Ensure the work lives on as a permanent public asset — documented clearly enough that the next contributor can build on it without starting over.

Who This Is For

  • Contribution over Curriculum A steep learning curve is a byproduct of hard work, not a prerequisite to starting.
  • Journey Already Underway Your personal projects and curiosities already live in AI, policy, or market-building. You do not need convincing.
  • Transdisciplinary Friction A coder who respects the law, a policy thinker who understands logic, or a designer who thinks in systems.
  • Legacy Mindset You want to leave a brick in the foundation — not a line on a resume, but a public asset that endures.

The Fellow does not leave with a certificate. They leave with a contribution — one permanent public asset that outlasts the programme and serves India's digital commons.
