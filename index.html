What We Enable

Two complementary
frameworks. One trusted architecture.

Both DEPA Training and DEPA Private Inferencing share a common foundation: data never leaves institutional boundaries without consent, purpose limitation, and cryptographic guarantees.

DEPA Training

The era of scraping public data to train AI is ending.

The next frontier demands access to high-quality datasets locked within institutional silos. DEPA Training unlocks AI model training on protected datasets — with privacy, sovereignty, and scalability built in from the start.

  • Confidential Clean Rooms — hardware-level isolation where models come to data, not data to models
  • Differential privacy with mathematical guarantees, not just anonymization
  • Electronic contracts with immutable audit trails on distributed ledgers
  • Compliant with DPDP Act, GDPR, and emerging AI regulations
Explore DEPA Training →
DEPA Private Inferencing

AI inference across institutional boundaries — without sharing raw data.

Privacy safeguards around sensitive institutional data remain onerous and fragile. DEPA Private Inferencing enables real-time AI inference on protected datasets without data ever leaving institutional boundaries.

  • Secure multi-party inference across organisations in real time
  • Consent-aware, purpose-limited data access at every step
  • Standardised contracts govern every cross-boundary inference event
  • Full provenance chain — from data principal to AI output

About Samyog

The governance layer
India's AI data ecosystem needs

Samyog is a stakeholder-led, self-regulatory organisation guiding the creation and management of Data Collaboratives — bringing together data providers, consumers, and service ecosystems to enable trusted, repeatable, multi-party data collaboration within focused domains.

It operationalises DEPA Training and Private Inferencing at scale by shaping market awareness and delivering critical governance services: certification, standardised contracts, key management infrastructure, grievance redressal, and training.

Powered by iSPIRT. Technical standards by DEPA Foundation.

What Samyog delivers
to the ecosystem

🏗️

Common Infrastructure

Shared platforms, onboarding systems, and key management infrastructure that power the DEPA ecosystem — removing the need for each participant to build from scratch.

✅

Certifications & Compliance

Entity registration, verification, and certification for data providers, consumers, and tech service providers — ensuring every participant meets the required standards.

📄

Standardised Smart Contracts

Machine-executable legal agreements and key management systems that make multi-party data collaborations legally sound, transparent, and auditable.

⚖️

Grievance Redressal

A formal dispute resolution mechanism that maintains trust across the ecosystem — providing clear recourse for all participants when things go wrong.

🎓

Training & Support

Technical and operational support for ecosystem participants — from onboarding to ongoing enablement — ensuring DEPA is adopted correctly and at scale.

📊

Data Catalogue & Discovery

A searchable registry of available datasets and their governance terms — enabling data consumers to discover and contract for the data they need responsibly.
